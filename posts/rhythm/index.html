Meaning of rhythm?

Rhythm describes how you choose to space notes in section of music through time. There are some standard rhythms that have names, but there is infinite number of rhythms.

Where can I find it?

As rhythm is very broad term, it applies to all parts of songs including harmony, melody and drums.

How to work with it?

I highly suggest you to just experiment and try even things that sound weird first. Rhythm has power to inspire melody, so maybe if you try something really strange, it will allow you to create some melody you could not even find ever before. Try to not snap every note to grid and leave enough spaces in between. Keeping spaces lets it breath and makes faster parts even more powerful. To execrices, do not plan it while improvizing and just let your had do, whatever it wants to do.
