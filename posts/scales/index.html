How does sound work?

You can look at sound like vibrations of air. The most simple sound from music viewpoint is sine wave. Sine wave looks like this:

What is interesting is that your instrument doesn’t play just sine-wave when you play one note. It plays many different sine-waves at once, but one is usually much stronger.

Hz (Hertz) measures how fast the air vibrates -> how many times it goes back and forth in one second.

Here you can see 1Hz tone that is much stronger than 3Hz tone. It combines into sound that is very similar to 1Hz sound. For that reason, we will now ignore these additional frequencies that are generated by your instrument and act like it just generates sinewave when you play one note.

Octave

An octave is just concept, that groups tones, their frequencies (Hz) are multiples of each other. Let’s say you have 2 tones, one is 4Hz and one is 8Hz, you can say that 8Hz is octave of 4Hz. It sounds like just useless terminology, but it is very important, because our mind interprets these frequencies as the same note, just either a bit “lower” or “higher”.

Interestingly, if you play these 2 at the same time, resulting wave very visibly shows characteristics of both and each cycle of lower one always ends on one of cycles of higher one, which might be the reason our brain sees them as same note.

12 equal temperament

And what about notes between octaves? In western music, we split frequencies between octaves into 12 tones that are named C, C#, D, D#, E, F, F#, G, G#, A, A#, B. I am starting from note C as it is standard, but it doesn’t really matter where you start. Only important thing is that all of these are always ordered like this.

This is the reason why we have multiple A notes on piano, each one belongs to different octave.

Hard to remember note names?
You can start with A, follow alphabet and when you get to G#, start again from A. Just remember that all notes have their sharp variant (# symbolizes sharp), except the notes E and B (Eat Banana).

This explains the 12 in concept of 12 equal temperament. The word temperament simply means how we split notes between octaves, and equal means we split them so that each note is the same distance from its neighbors.
