This guide will teach you how to understand music concepts that will help you improvise and experiment with music creation using any instrument. I will use mostly guitar to showcase this, but it applies everywhere. We will talk about intervals, scales, chords, rhythm, composition and much more.

We will be using so called “western music theory”, which is probably 99% of music you ever heard. You don’t have to worry about this, but it is always nice to check other views on music.
